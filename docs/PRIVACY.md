# PaySetu Privacy Policy & DPDP Act 2023 Compliance

## 1. Principles
PaySetu is designed in compliance with the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)** and Reserve Bank of India data localization guidelines:
- **Data Minimization**: Only information essential to processing payments and verifying KYC is requested.
- **Zero Sensitive Credential Storage**: Under no circumstances does PaySetu request, process, or store UPI PINs, CVVs, card PINs, banking passwords, or OTPs.
- **Masking Compliance**: All PAN records (`ABCDE****F`) and Aadhaar records (`XXXX-XXXX-4921`) are stored exclusively in masked formats.
- **Data Localization**: All payment transaction logs, user identifiers, and financial records reside exclusively within Indian data centers as mandated by the RBI directive on Storage of Payment System Data.
- **Right to Erasure**: Non-financial personal data can be requested for deletion, subject to statutory 10-year financial audit record retention requirements under PMLA and RBI guidelines.
